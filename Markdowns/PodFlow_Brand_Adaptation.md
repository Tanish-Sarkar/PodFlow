# PodFlow Brand Adaptation

PodFlow is an AI-powered multi-agent orchestration platform.

The UI should communicate:

- Intelligence
- Automation
- Trust
- Premium productivity

Use:

- Soft blue accents
- Glass workflow cards
- Floating agent status indicators
- Smooth pipeline visualizations
- Newsletter generation flow represented as connected glass nodes

Avoid:

- Corporate enterprise styling
- Dark heavy dashboards
- Excessive gradients
- Neon cyberpunk aesthetics

Target feeling:

"The future of knowledge extraction and content automation."
